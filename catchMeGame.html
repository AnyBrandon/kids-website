How to Play

Welcome to Catch Me If You Can! a smart, exciting and fun game where two players try to guess the secret character by using clues!

Goal:

Guess the target character before your opponent does!

How to Play:

  1. Start the Game: Click "Start Game" to pick a random character as the target.
  2. Take Turns: Players take turns revealing in secret one hint at a time about the target character. Each player gets 4 hints to help them figure out who the target is.
  3. Hints and Rarity: Hints tell you things like hair color, eye color, gender, accessories and many others. Some might be true, while others could be false rumors! Hints also have different rarities:
    • Common: Easy clues
    • Uncommon: More helpful clues
    • Rare: Big clues
  4. Guessing: After each hint, you can guess who the target character is. Type their name and click "Done." Guess wrong and you’re out.
  5. Examine the Board: After getting the clues, you get 20 seconds to look at the characters and remember details before making your guess.
  6. Final Guess: After both players have guessed, the game shows who was right!

Winning:

The first player to guess the correct character wins! If both players guess right, it’s a tie.
