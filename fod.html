How to Play: Fog of Darkzor

You and your opponent race across the mysterious grid to rescue the King Ado (👑).

Game Rules:

  • Move to any **adjacent** (up, down, left, right) tile on your turn.
  • The map is hidden! Tiles are only revealed when you step on them.
  • The first player to land on the **King (👑)** wins!

Tile Effects:

Tile Effect
⚡ Trap Stuns you, giving your opponent 2 consecutive turns.
💣 Bomb Sends you back to your starting position.
👟 Boot Grants you 1 extra move.
🌀 Teleport Allows you to jump to ANY open tile.
👤 Clone Swaps your position with your opponent's.
↔️ Expansion (Max 2) Adds a new 4-column section to the board! Gives you an extra turn.
❓ Clue Gives a hint about the King's proximity.
