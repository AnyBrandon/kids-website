How to Play

Crack the unique digit code before time runs out!

The Goal

You need to guess a secret code consisting of **unique digits** (0-9). The code length and difficulty depend on the level you select.

Feedback Guide

After you submit a guess, the history panel will give you feedback:

  • X P
    Correct Position (P): The digit is correct AND is in the right spot.
  • Y D
    Correct Digit (D): The digit is in the code, but in the wrong spot.

Collectible Cards (The Reward!)

Every time you successfully crack a code, you reveal a **Super Card** corresponding to that difficulty level (Common, Rare, Legendary, etc.).
All discovered cards are saved in the **Collectibles** section.

Collector's Challenge: If you manage to collect all the available cards (22), you will be awarded the ultimate **Money Prize** !
